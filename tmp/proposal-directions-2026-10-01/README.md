# SPACE Commercial Proposal — design exploration

Three finished one-page A4 directions. These are static design artifacts, not application or renderer changes. Each PDF has embedded fonts, selectable Ukrainian text, and a vector rendering of the existing SPACE logo. Each matching PNG is rendered from that PDF at 300 dpi.

Open **00-comparison.png** to compare the compositions side by side; open the individual PDFs to assess them at actual A4 size. The artwork itself has no concept labels or presentation framing.

## 01 — Measured Space / editorial architecture

- An offset serif project title creates an editorial voice, paired with the original SPACE identity.
- A narrow section rail and shared reading column suggest architectural datums without a visible grid.
- Warm near-white, graphite ink, and generous spacing supply character with very little print coverage.
- One large final price leads; discount, included VAT, and three payment rows remain clearly secondary.

Files: **01-measured-space.pdf** and **01-measured-space.png**.

## 02 — Quiet Monument / premium monochrome

- A centered logo and project title establish a calm, formal axis.
- The large price becomes the page’s typographic focal point, with a distinct but subordinate currency label.
- Pure white, black, and gray derive their richness from scale and spacing rather than decoration.
- A three-part pricing explanation and unboxed payment rows keep the lower page practical and readable.

Files: **02-quiet-monument.pdf** and **02-quiet-monument.png**.

## 03 — Folded Plane / SPACE geometry

- A pale mineral plane with one oblique edge draws its geometry from the SPACE logo’s angular A.
- A small triangular construction echoes the logo without changing or replacing the original brand asset.
- Geometric typography, an asymmetric price anchor, and numbered payment rows create a distinct architectural rhythm.
- The contained tone and generous white margins remain suitable for A4 printing and grayscale reading.

Files: **03-folded-plane.pdf** and **03-folded-plane.png**.

## Content flexibility

These mockups show the requested hard pricing state and three stages. The same reading order allows discount and VAT to be omitted independently, with the final price retaining its priority. Where an area tariff is unavailable, omit that basis and retain an object-area line if relevant, following the current proposal behavior. A fixed discount uses the same secondary line with its effective percentage. Stage notes can sit immediately below their stage name.

Two to four short payment rows can use the same cadence by adjusting the space before the schedule and note. Very long project titles, stage names, contact details, or notes need reflow and may require a second page, as in the current renderer; these representative one-page canvases are not an implementation specification for arbitrary maximum-length input.

Typography uses installed Ubuntu, IBM Plex Serif, IBM Plex Mono, and Jura fonts with Ukrainian glyph coverage. No new application assets or dependencies were added. The accompanying **design-philosophies.md** records the visual philosophies used by the canvas-design workflow.
